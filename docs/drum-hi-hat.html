Drum Hi-Hat

Drum Hi-Hat synthesizes an inharmonic metal cluster, a filtered wash, and a short closure chick. Closed, pedal, and open articulations share one metal identity but use independent decay behavior; closed and pedal hits choke an existing open tail.

Workflow

  1. Insert s3g Drum Hi-Hat 2 on a stereo instrument track and sequence notes 42, 44, and 46 for closed, pedal, and open hats.
  2. Route the track to the main stereo output or a headphone monitor.
  3. Set the metal color with TUNE, ALLOY, SPREAD, and DENSITY.
  4. Balance modal TONE against WASH, AIR, CHICK, and SIZZLE.
  5. Adjust the three decay controls and CHOKE TIME while playing an open-to-closed pattern.

Sound Model

ALLOY and SPREAD move the oscillator cluster away from regular harmonic spacing; DENSITY fills the metal field. TONE weights its bands, while AIR, WASH, and SIZZLE build the noisy cymbal layer around it.

The three articulations alter excitation and duration, not just gain. Open hits retain the longest metal and wash response. Closed hits use the closed decay and terminate an open voice over CHOKE TIME. Pedal hits emphasize the closure chick and use their own decay.

MIDI Articulations

MIDI RECEIVE defaults to OMNI; choose CH 1 through CH 16 to accept only that channel when several drum instruments share one sequencer stream.

NoteArticulationBehavior
42Closed Hi-HatShort metal; chokes open voices.
44Pedal Hi-HatFoot-closure emphasis; chokes open voices.
46Open Hi-HatLong metal and wash decay.

Other notes use the closed articulation and transpose the metal according to NOTE TRACKING. The named notes are exposed to hosts through the CLAP note-name extension.

Presets and Safe Random

The fourteen presets include BALANCED ALLOY, TIGHT CIRCUIT, OPEN CURRENT, PEDAL CHICK, DARK TAPE, and WIDE SIZZLE. RANDOM generates a playable metal-and-wash family while preserving OUTPUT GAIN, NOTE TRACKING, and VELOCITY SENSITIVITY.

Output

STEREO WIDTH distributes upper metal and wash detail while maintaining a stable center. Set it to 0% for exact dual mono.
