Drum Toms

Drum Toms places low, mid, and high synthesized toms in one correlated rack. The three drums keep independent tuning anchors but share their membrane, shell, pitch-fall, rim, stick, and character design, making it practical to reshape a complete family as one instrument.

Workflow

  1. Insert s3g Drum Toms on a stereo instrument track and sequence notes 45, 48, and 50 for the low, mid, and high heads.
  2. Route the track to the main stereo output or a headphone monitor.
  3. Set LOW TUNE, MID TUNE, and HIGH TUNE as an ordered family.
  4. Shape their common shell and decay contour, then audition the corresponding rim notes.
  5. Open STEREO WIDTH if the low-to-high rack should spread across the image.

Sound Model

Each slot runs the same membrane-and-shell voice used by the floor tom, with slot-specific damping, seed identity, and stereo position. DECAY SPREAD changes the low-to-high lifetime contour around the common BODY DECAY. Positive values let the lower drum ring longer; negative values reverse that contour.

The rack's rim notes excite a tonal rim and short stick band at the matching head pitch. RIM CHARACTER, RIM DECAY, and STICK TONE therefore reshape all three articulations without destroying their family resemblance.

MIDI Articulations

MIDI RECEIVE defaults to OMNI; choose CH 1 through CH 16 to accept only that channel when several drum instruments share one sequencer stream.

Presets and Safe Random

The twelve factory racks include BALANCED RACK, DEEP THREE, ASCENDING WOOD, RIM KNOCKS, ELECTRIC FALL, and WIDE RACK. RANDOM generates ordered, correlated low/mid/high tunings along with a safe common voice. It preserves OUTPUT GAIN, NOTE TRACKING, and VELOCITY SENSITIVITY.

Output

Width places low toward the left, mid at center, and high toward the right while retaining coherent mono collapse. At 0%, the two output channels are identical.
