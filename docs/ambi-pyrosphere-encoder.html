Ambi Encoder Pyrosphere

s3g Ambi Encoder Pyrosphere is the thermal counterpart to Cryosphere: a zero-input geological instrument in which combustion acts on fuel, structures, and rock. It turns heat release, pyrolysis, thermal stress, fracture, spall, pressure, debris, and plume transport into a first-through-seventh-order ACN/SN3D field.

Thermal-Geology Model

The source model is aperiodic. Each field voice maintains a heat reservoir and stochastic combustion layer while its material accumulates thermal stress. Threshold crossings launch branching fracture cascades, surface-spall bursts, fragment showers, pressure releases, and occasional collapse events. Standing timber and forest materials add three independently loaded structural generations: trunk, limbs, and fine branches. Wind and plume motion drive Strouhal-scaled shedding forces; heat damage lowers failure resistance until bright branch snaps cascade into trunk rupture, a hinged descent, and a separate ground impact. Broadband and filtered-noise impulses provide ember and fire-patch texture without a persistent pitched oscillator bed.

MATERIAL changes heat uptake, combustion continuity, brittleness, fracture likelihood, spall behavior, debris mass, and spectral loss. Combustion and structural choices include GAS, WICK / WAX, DUFF / PEAT, TIMBER, COAL, OIL, MASONRY, METAL, EMBERS, RESIN, GRASS, FOREST, ROCK / TALUS, and PRESSURE JET. The planetary materials—SILICATE CELLS, SUPERCRITICAL FRONT, SULFUR SLUG VENTS, and THERMOELASTIC LATTICE—select separate non-combustion process models.

PRESSURE JET activates a dedicated continuous source for flamethrower-like behavior. Correlated low bands form the plenum and jet core; progressively decorrelated bands form the turbulent shear layer and nozzle hiss; a lagged downstream source follows unsteady heat release. PRESSURE and HEAT establish jet drive, while ROAR, HISS, OXYGEN, PYROLYSIS, and combustion color shape the layers. This source is an acoustic inference from turbulent non-premixed-flame research rather than a computational-fluid-dynamics simulation.

The planetary model avoids the flame bed, pressure-jet nozzle, structural fall, and low collapse-body sine. SILICATE CELLS couples viscosity-lagged convective reservoirs, stochastic blisters, and brittle surface skins. SUPERCRITICAL FRONT treats reaction as folded density and pressure sheets under strong state-dependent absorption. SULFUR SLUG VENTS cycle a shared pressure reservoir through choking, two-phase slug release, and foamy resealing. THERMOELASTIC LATTICE accumulates metastable domain stress, then propagates defect fronts and broadband avalanches. A completed blister, reaction front, slug, or lattice release can briefly energize two scattered thermal-material modes per entity. Their finite stochastic force and distributed tuning create alien cavity and mineral behavior without a tuned impact strike, bass drum body, or autonomous resonator bed.

HEAT, IGNITION RATE, FLARE DEPTH, ROAR, FLICKER, PYROLYSIS, OXYGEN, and CHAR control heat release. PYROLYSIS lowers the combustion-body filters as it adds mass, reaching the pressure-heavy sub and low-mid region as well as darkening the upper bands. The lowest plume and pressure bands are DC-safe and energy-compensated, so lowering their cutoff retains audible weight; collapse and tree-impact events additionally excite short material-dependent ground/body impulses rather than a permanent bass oscillator. IGNITION RATE is a direct logarithmic clock from 2 mHz to 12 Hz: it schedules heat-release changes and proportionally paces thermal damage, fragments, and structural consequences. Intermediate values are displayed in millihertz below 1 Hz, and FLICKER changes instability without altering the event rate. PLUME WANDER is a logarithmic spatial clock from 1 mHz to 0.5 Hz; it is interpolated geometrically on SURF and contributes directly to field motion. BRITTLENESS, COMB DETAIL, FRAGMENTS, PLUME VORTEX, and PRESSURE control material failure and transport. The STRUCT LOAD, BRANCH SNAP, and TREE FALL controls govern accumulated wind/heat loading, the high-frequency detail and branching of rupture, and the probability and weight of the final hinge/fall/impact consequence. Structural activity receives a foreground mix: flame energy ducks briefly around snaps, rupture, descent, and ground impact so those consequences remain intelligible inside dense combustion. The model draws on fire-sound decomposition, hierarchical tree-motion sound generation, and experimental acoustic-emission work on thermally shocked rock; it is a realtime procedural interpretation, not a finite-element fire simulation or a recording player. Aleatoric entity score

Each field voice is a finite entity with available fuel, structural integrity, and ember charge rather than a permanently active flame. A deterministic aleatoric score moves entities through rest, heat accumulation, ignition, propagation, consequence, and aftermath. Ignition can travel to the nearest field entities after a distance-dependent delay; burning consumes fuel, heat weakens standing material, structural failure distributes embers, and the score returns depleted entities to a real rest state. The pressure-jet material is the deliberate continuous exception.

PACE moves from long geological arcs to rapid event turnover. OCCUPANCY sets the target number of simultaneous entities, while homeostasis prevents the complete field from remaining permanently active. CASCADE controls spatial transfer and causal branching. MEMORY makes successive macro-scenes retain or change their activity pattern. REST lengthens aftermaths and removes the residual combustion bed between arcs. The four planetary materials span lone remote cells and deep rests through full-field colonies and long propagation chains; the other materials follow their defined score response. These controls affect triggers and physical state rather than applying an amplitude gate to the completed mix.

Presets and SURF

Eighteen factory scenes establish distinct process regions: Duff Smoulder, Timber Pyrolysis, Resinous Brush, Grass Front, Forest Combustion Front, Root Burn, Coal Seam, Oil Pool, Masonry Thermal Spall, Rock Talus Spall, Structural Collapse, Firestorm Debris Field, Burned Timber Fall, Flamethrower Jet, Silicate Convection Sea, Dense-Atmosphere Hydrocarbon Front, Sulfur Vent Colony, and Mineral Lattice Collapse. The last four use dedicated planetary physics, emphasizing unfamiliar matter, atmosphere, collective pressure exchange, and nonhuman timescales rather than conventional weapon effects.

On the viewport’s SURF page, ADD stores the current preset as a cell and CAP overwrites the selected cell. In PLAY, the automatable Surface X/Surface Y cursor interpolates continuous parameters, including the five score controls; discrete fuel, place, and response choices use the nearest cell. VOICES stays an integer readout while surplus combustion lanes fade continuously in both synthesis and the field drawing. CURVE, FOCUS, and GLIDE shape the morph; POP opens the synchronized surface in an attached utility panel.

Routing

The output bus is always 64 channels; ORDER activates the first 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, or 64 ACN channels.

Follow it with an Ambi Decoder Speaker, Ambi Decoder Stereo, Ambi Decoder Head, or Analyzer Ambi Energy.
