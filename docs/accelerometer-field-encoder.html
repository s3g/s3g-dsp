Ambi Encoder Modal

Ambi Encoder Modal is a multi-body modal instrument that publishes first-, second-, or third-order ACN/SN3D directly. It creates four to eight independent resonant bodies, gives each one its own evolving modal state and continuous actuator history, and places the resulting ensemble in an editable spatial field. The bodies are deliberately fictional musical archetypes rather than claims to reproduce named or calibrated instruments.

The instrument keeps its synthesis cost predictable with one fixed pool of 96 modes. Four active bodies receive 24 modes each; eight receive 12 each; intermediate body counts divide the same pool in complete pairs. Increasing the population therefore trades some detail within each body for a denser and more varied ensemble instead of multiplying CPU use.

Signal Flow

  1. The LISTENER / ACTUATOR, mono Modal Actuator In, and MIDI note-ons feed the resonant banks themselves. There is no autonomous event generator, dry actuator feed, contact click, noise burst, or separate transient layer.
  2. The fixed TETRA 4 or CUBE 8 listener samples the internal HOA field independently of the editable body constellation. Its directional history helps route bounded continuous drive to one body or across the ensemble.
  3. The fixed 96-mode pool is divided among the active bodies. Close modal pairs produce beating, while COUPLING animates their sustained amplitude and frequency relationships.
  4. Each body produces its own contact-to-radiation mixture before spatial projection. Per-body variation prevents an ensemble from becoming several copies of one resonator.
  5. BODY SPREAD distributes the body centers around the selected azimuth and elevation. Per-body AED offsets and distance can then be edited directly before the resulting directions are encoded as ACN/SN3D.
  6. In DIFFUSE + DRONE, the Listener/Actuator combines underexposed listener directions with each body’s slow energy deficit, maintaining a steady distributed field without returning decoded audio to the resonators.
  7. The normal output is HOA. Optional 8 BODY STEMS publishes one post-body, pre-HOA signal per lane and clears lanes above the active body count.
  8. On the HOA path, LIFT adds slow modal-aware makeup without entering the resonators or Listener analysis. Smoothed OUT then trims the complete result, followed by one linked peak guard shared by every active channel.

The family choice is a starting distribution, not an identity. The bronze, laminate, glass, metal, ceramic, and wood entries combine broad material-loss tendencies with an abstract plate or shell form. The five membrane entries instead describe resonant construction: tension, loading, a second skin, a cavity, or uneven low tension can be combined conceptually with many possible skin materials. Body index, deterministic variation, size, and coupling perturb every distribution independently, so an eight-body field contains eight related resonators rather than eight unison copies. Published measurements provide useful information about beating, differentiated damping, modal coupling, and changing radiation, but do not make any body a model of the measured specimen or a named drum.

MIDI and Actuator Input

The CLAP exposes one note input named Modal Actuator MIDI In. A note-on selects and smoothly energizes a modal body: velocity scales that modal-only envelope through a soft high-velocity knee, while pitch offsets the addressed body around C4 / MIDI note 60. Coincident notes use idle bodies before replacing an active pulse. Each body retains a short, leaky memory of recently admitted strike energy, so a full-strength actuation temporarily consumes its headroom and dense retriggers are reduced or deferred until that headroom recovers. This conditioning occurs before the resonators and is separate from the linked output guard. It does not add a click, pop, dry onset, or noise burst. Note-off does not truncate an already resonating body. CLAP note events and conventional MIDI note-on messages are both accepted, and their frame offsets are honored within each audio block.

MIDI and Modal Actuator In remain additive. The mono input can carry control envelopes, contact signals, or audio, but it reaches the selected modal banks only and is never mixed dry to the output. The Listener/Actuator supplies the continuous internal drive; with DIFFUSE + DRONE, it moves energy toward quiet bodies and underexposed directions without phase-locking them.

Spatial Body Editor

The field view follows the camera vocabulary shared by the repository’s other encoders. TOP and SIDE are orthographic editing views; 3/4 supplies the spatial overview. Drag a body directly in TOP or SIDE to change its AED position and distance. In 3/4, clicking a body selects it. From any camera, dragging blank field space orbits into a free view. The minus and plus buttons change camera scale, and RESET clears every manual body offset and returns all body distances to 1.0.

The selected body also exposes automatable AZ OFFSET, EL OFFSET, and DISTANCE controls in the BODY AED panel. These manual values are additive to Field Azimuth, Field Elevation, and Body Spread, so the global constellation remains useful after point editing.

Body color uses the same perceptual AED distinction as the other spatial editors: in OKLCH, hue follows azimuth, lightness follows elevation, and chroma follows distance. The body’s base color therefore remains a stable position readout. Live modal energy is shown only by the surrounding halo; it does not replace the AED color.

The neutral diamonds labeled E1–E4 or E1–E8 are the fixed listener pickups. Their connecting shell follows the camera but not body AED edits; each gray halo shows what that pickup currently hears. Actuator routes use actual modal-drive telemetry, with moving diamonds showing force traveling from the listener relationship toward the bodies. In DIFFUSE mode several routes can remain active at once. These ears and routes are analysis/control displays, not additional audio outputs.

Model Basis

The core is a compact population of modal banks. Each mode has a frequency, exponential decay, actuation weight, pickup weight, and radiation weight; each body owns the state of the modes assigned to it, its conditioning path, and one independently encoded HOA direction. Small frequency differences within a pair produce beating, while body-to-body variation creates slower ensemble motion. The fixed budget is intentionally allocated across bodies rather than rendering a full modal bank at every position.

The plate-family centers begin with normalized thin-plate relationships. For an isotropic plate, bending stiffness is D = Eh³ / (12(1 − ν²)), and a simply supported rectangular family follows ωmn = π² sqrt(D / ρh) ((m/a)² + (n/b)²). Carbon Laminate and Spruce Plate use the corresponding orthotropic form, in which D11 kx⁴ + 2(D12 + 2D66) kx²ky² + D22 ky⁴ replaces the isotropic stiffness term. Steel and porcelain use separate shell-like curvature branches rather than reusing those plate ratios. The resulting centers are normalized and paired with small asymmetries: they define stable musical archetypes without asserting specimen dimensions or measured boundary conditions.

The membrane centers use tension-dominated relationships rather than plate bending stiffness. For an ideal circular membrane of radius a, tension per unit length T, and areal density σ, ωmn = (jmn / a) sqrt(T / σ), where jmn is a zero of the Bessel function Jm. Modes with angular order m > 0 occur as nominally degenerate sine/cosine pairs; small tension or boundary asymmetry splits those pairs and makes their beating audible. Loaded Membrane changes selected effective modal masses, while Loose Membrane exaggerates split pairs and differentiated losses to abstract uneven low tension. These are normalized modal lattices, not full finite-element membrane solvers.

Coupled Membrane uses two slightly mismatched lattices with anti-phase paired modulation, abstracting the acoustical energy exchange measured between two drumheads. Cavity Membrane adds air-loaded branches and altered decay/radiation weighting: enclosed air can move different membrane modes by different amounts and change their decay times. Membrane profiles also apply a small, bounded actuator-dependent tension lift that relaxes with the driving force; it is strongest in Loose Membrane and never adds modes to the fixed pool. None of these profiles synthesizes a snare wire, shell impact, stick transient, or recorded drum attack; the existing MIDI, external input, and Listener/Actuator paths energize the resonant banks directly.

Material loss is represented independently of modal spacing. Every family supplies a base e-folding time and frequency-dependent decay falloff, then DAMP rescales that trajectory. A configured decay time τ becomes the discrete pole radius r = exp(−1 / (τFs)); its equivalent 60 dB decay is T60 = ln(1000) τ. Family-specific drive scale and radiation corner/exponent further distinguish how structural motion reaches the contact and air paths. These controls capture broad measured tendencies—such as longer glass and steel tails or stronger upper loss in porous ceramic and spruce—without treating a material name as a complete physical object.

Additive-resynthesis and nonlinear-plate studies show the value of selected spectral landmarks, differentiated modal envelopes, synchronized slow/fast AM and FM, frequency-dependent loss, coupling, and gradual spectral energy transfer. Ambi Encoder Modal recombines those general behaviors into fictional sustained bodies and bounded real-time trajectories rather than claiming to solve or identify a particular instrument.

Modal identity and encoded direction remain separate. HOA Body Spread changes only where the independent body centers appear: at minimum they coincide at the selected field center; increasing it opens the ensemble into a rotated Fibonacci constellation. Manual body AED offsets and distance are applied after that generated placement. None of these spatial operations detune the bodies or widen a single mono source, and spatial geometry interpolates so that moving the ensemble remains playable.

The contact and radiation protection curves now remain at one fixed internal calibration: OUT no longer drives them, so raising the level does not harden the body or change the contact-to-radiation color. Continuously driven modes add smooth amplitude-dependent pole damping only when displacement begins to accumulate beyond the normal range; long decays therefore settle without a hard state recovery for Lift to expose as a click. LIFT observes body-domain power independently of ambisonic order, moves downward quickly for a newly loud body, rises slowly, and freezes its upward motion near silence rather than pulling up the end of a modal decay. It can supply up to 18 dB at full setting, while its default setting is deliberately moderate. Optional body stems bypass Lift to preserve their direct ensemble balance.

OUT is a 25 ms-smoothed gain after modal formation and spatial encoding. The final output stage uses one thresholded linked guard with a −1 dBFS sample ceiling, immediate reduction, and a 300 ms release. It applies the same scalar to every active ACN/SN3D coefficient—or every body stem—so spatial direction and ensemble ratios remain intact. Below the threshold it introduces no new gain reduction; unlike the former instantaneous soft-safety curve, it does not waveshape every sample.

The Listener remains causal control, not an audio feedback return. Its world-fixed Tetra 4 or Cube 8 body analyzes the fixed-calibration internal HOA field before Lift, Out, peak protection, and the output-mode branch, independent of body count, Field Center, Body Spread, and manual AED edits. The actuator uses those directional scores together with per-body energy to apply bounded sustaining force. Its slow and fast stochastic force components use fixed physical-time bandwidths rather than sample-dependent coefficients; this retains irregular pressure while keeping broadband rub from becoming a wire-like vibration in the long bronze modes. Changing audition level therefore cannot redirect the actuator. DIFFUSE routing with DRONE continuously favors bodies and directions below the ensemble’s target exposure without flattening the beating and decay that make them distinct. This behavior remains available when the host receives body stems.

Presets

The 25 sustained presets preserve the original bank—DEEP FIELD, QUIET ALLOY, TIERED CONSTELLATION, PAIRED FIELD, DENSE CONSTELLATION, SLOW ORBIT, BRIGHT DRIFT, ALLOY CURRENT, BRIGHT FOCUS, MUTED BLOOM, BROAD WIND, BRIGHT HALO, and SLOW CEREMONY—and append CARBON VEIL, GLASS HORIZON, STEEL CANOPY, ALUMINUM CURRENT, PORCELAIN ORBIT, EARTHEN BLOOM, SPRUCE BREATH, TENSION VEIL, LOADED DRIFT, COUPLED CURRENT, CAVITY BREATH, and LOOSE HORIZON. Deep Field remains the default. Every preset describes a continuous playing condition and modal tendency, not an instrument specimen or a library of artificial attacks.

CLAP and Renderer Workflow

The bundle is s3g_ambi_encoder_modal.clap and appears in hosts as s3g Ambi Encoder Modal 16. Its native 1160 × 760 interface uses the shared encoder title actions—Preset, Load, Save, and guarded Random—with Output first and separate Modal Body, Listener / Actuator, Projection, Body Character, Body AED, and Signal Path panels beside the editable camera view.

cmake --preset clap
cmake --build --preset clap --target s3g_accelerometer_field_encoder_clap
./scripts/install-clap-bundles.sh

cmake --build --preset dev --target \
  s3g_accelerometer_field_encoder_smoke \
  s3g_accelerometer_field_encoder_render
./build/s3g_accelerometer_field_encoder_render --list

Limits

The body families are compact musical profiles, not calibrated replicas. The engine does not solve full two-dimensional geometry, identify an alloy or construction method, or run the hundreds of modes required for a high-energy nonlinear plate simulation. Its fixed pool also means that eight-body settings deliberately carry fewer modes per body than four-body settings. Modal Coupling and the continuous Listener/Actuator reproduce selected audible consequences within a bounded real-time instrument.
