Ambisonic Effects

Ambisonic effects transform a complete encoded field while preserving the relationships among its channels. Place these after an encoder or ambisonic instrument and before the final decoder.

Selected effects and spatial instruments also use Listener Mode, an internal analysis layer that lets an ambisonic field influence its own later processing.

Field Effects

  • Ambi Effect DJ Filter: an order-adaptive 1OA through 7OA low-pass/open/high-pass sweep with per-pickup variation, input meters, and directional masking across Icosa12, Dodeca20, or Sphere24 listener bodies.
  • Ambi Effect Delay: overlap-add per-pickup delay time and feedback, with macro spread/deviation, topology routing, radial meters, and a directional dry/wet mask.
  • Ambi Effect Pitch: dual-grain per-pickup pitch and window relationships using the same upscaled body, listener view, topology, and mask.
  • Ambi Effect Gain: a neutral-at-zero directional level field with per-pickup gain, macro relationships, topology, and dry-suppressing mask.
  • Ambi Effect Displacement: order-adaptive time-varying field warps loaded from 24-point Displacement Score geometry and resampled onto the selected listener body.
  • Ambi Imprint: directional convolution using .s3gimprint space responses.
  • Ambi Rotate: coherent first-through-seventh-order field rotation.

Field and Bus Effects

  • Ambi Depth: depth, focus, air, tail, low-body, and width shaping for one third-order field.
  • Ambi Field / Bus Effects: group matrix, node mixing, rotation, and depth across lane-locked third-order feeds.
  • Processor Ambi Grain: loaded-media granulation with one shared grain event stream across all active ambisonic channels.

Signal-chain Rule

encoder or encoded media
-> ambisonic effects
-> decoder
-> speaker or stereo output

Do not treat individual ambisonic channels as unrelated stems. Use the field-aware processors here when timing, filtering, geometry, or gain changes must remain coherent across the encoding.
